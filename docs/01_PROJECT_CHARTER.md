## Phased priority

**Phase 1 - now to ~90 days: stability**
Get a stable job (IT or adjacent), continue freelancing, keep the AI agency (Venture #16) running in parallel. A job funds everything else - stability, credit history, capital for later ventures.

**Phase 2 - once income is stable: agency growth**
Grow the Web & App Agency (Venture #16) as the primary revenue engine and live proving ground for BVOS.

**Phase 3 - full BVOS**
Expand into the full venture registry as capacity allows.

## Operating system split

- **Personal Operating System (POS)** - runs the founder: career, finance, learning, personal admin
- **Agency Operating System (AOS)** - runs the Web & App Agency: sales, client success, product discovery, design, dev, QA, deployment, marketing
- **Venture Operating System (VOS)** - runs every future company (Shiftlyx, Finance OS, insurance, real estate, marketplace, SaaS, AI company), each plugging into shared departments rather than getting dedicated agents
