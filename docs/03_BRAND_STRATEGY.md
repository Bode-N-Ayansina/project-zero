# Brand Strategy

## Identity layers

**Legal identity**: Nurudeen Bode Ayansina
Used for: government documents, legal contracts, company registration, banking, tax records, official certifications, job applications, background checks.

**Public/professional brand**: Bode N. Ayansina
Used for: GitHub, LinkedIn, X, personal website, conference talks, publications, email signature, pitch decks, portfolio.

## Why the split is safe

Name-format mismatches only matter at the legal/background-check stage of hiring, where the SSN/ID/work-authorization name has to match exactly. Recruiters browsing a resume or clicking a portfolio link don't cross-reference name formatting - they just look at the work. To remove any ambiguity, the resume header can note both: "Nurudeen Bode Ayansina (Bode N. Ayansina - GitHub/LinkedIn)".
